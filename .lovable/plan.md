# Complete researched city-page coverage

## Target
- Reach at least **100 Indian city pages**, preserving the existing five Gulf pages.
- Bring every location page to **3,000+ substantive words**, measured from its primary content rather than menus, footer or hidden metadata.
- Write original, natural, readable copy. Do not claim human authorship, guaranteed plagiarism-free coverage, or guaranteed search rankings.
- Keep the current visual identity and fast-loading pages. Do not publish.

## Phase 1 — Evidence and editorial briefs
- Confirm existing route and word-count coverage and identify unsupported assertions in older city content.
- Research at least 18 additional Indian cities using authoritative city, industry and government sources.
- Reuse the existing Semrush keyword research where relevant; label unmeasured city-level demand as unknown.
- Record sources, local business distinctions, buyer questions and service priorities for every city.
- Prioritize smaller-city markets. Treat tier labels as planning categories, not official universal classifications; do not relabel cities simply to manufacture a 30% metro ratio.

## Phase 2 — Original long-form editorial batches
- Expand existing city pages and add researched new cities in reviewed batches.
- Each page needs distinct local use cases, service selection, SEO/content and paid-search planning, website/software options, CRM and AI workflow considerations, enquiry qualification, measurement, implementation steps and FAQs.
- Add practical detail appropriate to that city’s business audience rather than repeating a generic service essay with a changed city name.
- Do not add invented offices, local clients, testimonials, auction prices, rankings or results. Retain confirmed agency-wide facts without presenting them as city results.
- Include readable headings, short paragraphs, meaningful internal links and enquiry calls to action. Use existing visuals where suitable; source or generate additional imagery only where it improves understanding.

## Phase 3 — Integration and speed
- Keep editorial data separate from the shared location layout, and expose routes only for complete editorial records.
- Load substantial editorial text in city-specific groups rather than adding all expanded articles to the homepage bundle.
- Add a lightweight contents navigation for long pages and preserve useful mobile enquiry access without overlapping floating controls.
- Use remote service-area schema rather than fictional branches; keep visible FAQs and structured answers consistent.
- Extend the existing sitemap mechanism and verify crawler access without unrelated changes.

## Phase 4 — Quality gates
- Count primary page words across every location route; require at least 3,000 per completed page.
- Check exact duplicate paragraphs and near-duplicate passages across the city corpus, excluding unavoidable brand/contact text. Review flagged pairs instead of merely paraphrasing them.
- Check every factual local assertion against its recorded source. Internal comparison cannot establish originality against the entire web; any external plagiarism check must be reported separately with its actual coverage.
- Run relevant tests and inspect automatically generated build results.
- Check every location route on mobile and desktop for errors, overflow, headings, canonical URLs, schema, imagery and links; exercise representative enquiry paths.

## Completion report
Report actual city totals, measured word counts, overlap results, source coverage, performance evidence and any unavailable checks. Keep incomplete items open rather than claiming completion.
